English

HL / FAL · Paper 1–3

Focus areas for the finals: comprehension & summary technique, language structures, essay and transactional writing, and unseen poetry. Set-work literature (novel/drama) essays follow the same planning method as the discursive essay below — practise that structure and apply it to your prescribed text.

Mathematics

Paper 1 & 2

Paper 1 leans on algebra, sequences, finance, functions and calculus. Paper 2 leans on geometry, trig, statistics and probability. Marks are won on method — always show every step, even when you're sure of the answer.

Physical Sciences

Paper 1 (Physics) · Paper 2 (Chemistry)

Paper 1 is mechanics, electricity and waves. Paper 2 is chemical change and organic chemistry. Units and significant figures cost easy marks if you rush — always finish an answer with the correct unit.

Life Sciences

Paper 1 & 2

Paper 1 usually covers DNA/genetics, evolution and human reproduction; Paper 2 covers the nervous & endocrine systems, homeostasis and human impact on the environment (this can shift slightly by year, so check your own province's exam guideline). Diagrams carry real marks — label neatly and learn the flow of each process, not just isolated facts.

Geography

Paper 1 (Theory) · Paper 2 (Maps & Skills)

Paper 1 is theory (climate/weather, geomorphology, rural & urban settlement, economic geography). Paper 2 tests map work, GIS concepts and calculations (scale, gradient, orientation) using a topographic and orthophoto map. Practise the calculation methods until they're automatic — they're guaranteed, easy marks if you know the formula.
